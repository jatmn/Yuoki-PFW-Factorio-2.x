Version: 0.5.1
Date: 9. 10. 2026
  Changes:
    - Reuse Engines' Trade Node and Yuoki's minigun, energy ammunition, mobile generator, movement equipment and first walker.
    - Allow the Engines Trade Node to process PFW trades while retaining its existing categories and operating statistics.
    - Keep redundant PFW definitions commented with parent ownership references; retain all distinct recipes and quantities.
    - Reuse Yuoki's Lasergun for both old energy guns, CF-35 for Reactive/Shielded Armor, CF-35 for CF-56, and KT-60 for KT-34; preserve all export quantities and payouts for later balancing.
    - Restore Combat Armor Mk.1 manufacture: two refined N4 and four iron plates produce two components in one second in the War Material Factory.
    - Disable only the PFW energy cell's wearable-battery function; use Yuoki's existing battery for wearable storage. Preserve the cell's fuel value, all recipes, commented equipment code and artwork.
    - Keep the second-armor mapping pending; preserve unique original artwork and other disabled content.
  Graphics:
    - Include the original Mod Portal thumbnail in the package for the in-game mod browser.
    - Replace 41 confirmed duplicate artwork files with references to Yuoki Industries or Yuoki Engines, including the shared-factory icon and animation.
    - Use Engines' animation for the Heavy Weapons, Tank and Supply factories in all directions, including their fixed in-game view; retain the original alternate sheet for future use.
    - Generate import/export arrows with Yuoki helpers instead of 49 precomposed arrow-icon variants.
    - Refresh retained factory, display, commodity, weapon, material, armor and support-item icons with AI-assisted artwork based on original sprites.
    - Match the empty and filled energy-cell icons to one model; retain the energy-support unit's box and side-mounted cylinder.
    - Redraw the crafting-tab icon at 128px; preserve the 128px energy-cell equipment image for future use.
    - Redraw both static profit-display world sprites at double resolution while preserving their display size and placement.
    - Use one neutral left-hand body and shared moving parts for weapons, vehicle, equipment and component factories, with independent material and light tints and separate right-hand assemblies.
    - Generate those four factory icons from the same shared parts and palettes as their world sprites.
    - Keep rotor hubs and housings fixed; move paired lights with their cutouts, preserve rear housing occlusion and use a complete front-cylinder face.
    - Provide a separate, configurable opening-glow overlay, initially enabled for the component factory.
    - Rebuild the component factory's six-cell production cycle with a fixed platform and matching cells; include shadows between cells and onto the platform without stray shadow fragments.
    - Keep the ammunition factory's distinct body and paired front/rear drum animation, with separate moving parts and shadow.
    - Rebuild the cyborg factory with fixed vat shells and a separate green-to-pink fill/drain animation.
    - Preserve the original 16-frame animation cycles, including the slower cyborg and component factory speeds.
  Bugfixes:
    - Match 48 primary manufacturing recipe IDs to their products and set main products for names and Factoriopedia grouping.
    - Put those products in their manufacturing subgroups; keep imports, exports and alternate recipes distinct.
    - Supply missing recipe and armor names; update gun and shield export labels to their current parent items.
    - Allow the War Material Factory to craft the six-ingredient Advanced Targeting Device recipe.
  Compatibility:
    - Use the parent gun and shields with their existing equipment statistics, ammunition and manufacturing recipes.
    - Keep migrations deferred until a formal release; validate this development version with fresh games.
