    - Redraw both static profit-display world sprites at double resolution while preserving their display size and placement.

    - Generate those four factory icons from the same shared parts and palettes as their world sprites.
    - Keep rotor hubs and housings fixed; move paired lights with their cutouts, preserve rear housing occlusion and use a complete front-cylinder face.
    - Provide a separate, configurable opening-glow overlay, initially enabled for the component factory.
    - Rebuild the component factory's six-cell production cycle with a fixed platform and matching cells; include shadows between cells and onto the platform without stray shadow fragments.
    - Keep the ammunition factory's distinct body and paired front/rear drum animation, with separate moving parts and shadow.
    - Rebuild the cyborg factory with fixed vat shells and a separate green-to-pink fill/drain animation.
    - Preserve the original 16-frame animation cycles, including the slower cyborg and component factory speeds.
  Compatibility:
    - Keep the two pending PFW energy guns compatible with Yuoki's energy ammunition.
    - Keep migrations deferred until a formal release; validate this development version with fresh games.
